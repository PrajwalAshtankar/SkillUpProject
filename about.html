About SkillUp

Empowering careers. Creating opportunities. Changing lives.

SkillUp Coalition is a nonprofit organization dedicated to helping individuals build successful careers without the need for a traditional college degree. We focus on high-opportunity career pathways that offer livable wages and long-term growth.

Our mission is to support job seekers from start to finish—by helping them explore career options, gain relevant skills, and connect with quality job opportunities.

Our Mission

To empower individuals with clear, accessible, and affordable pathways to meaningful employment—especially for those without a college degree.

Our Vision

A future where everyone has equal access to high-quality careers, economic stability, and lifelong learning opportunities.

What We Do

Career Exploration

Discover non-degree career paths with strong job growth.

Skill Development

Learn job-ready skills through trusted training programs.

Job Connections

Access quality jobs that don’t require a college degree.

Ongoing Support

Receive guidance, coaching, and resources throughout your journey.

Why Choose SkillUp?

  • No degree required pathways
  • Trusted training partners
  • Real job opportunities
  • Nonprofit, learner-first approach

At SkillUp, we believe talent exists everywhere—opportunity should too. Our goal is to make career success achievable for everyone.
